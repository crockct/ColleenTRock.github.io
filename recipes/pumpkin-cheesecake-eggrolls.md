Ingredients

24 egg rolls

 16 oz cream cheese

 ½ cup granulated sugar

 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

 2 (16 oz) packages pre-made egg roll wrappers

 8oz pumpkin butter

 4 quarts oil (for frying)

Directions

1.  Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed [pan](https://amzn.to/34Cephh) over
    medium-high heat until it reaches 350°-375°. Meanwhile, prepare the
    egg rolls.

2.  In a medium bowl, beat the cream cheese, sugar, vanilla extract and
    pumpkin butter until smooth. It should be pretty thick.

3.  Scoop 2 tablespoons of filling into the center of the egg roll
    wrappers. **DO NOT OVERSTUFF. 2 TBS was pushing it!**

4.  Wet the edges of the egg roll wrapper, fold in the sides and the
    roll, pressing the edges to close. Repeat with the remaining filling
    and wrappers.

5.  Once the oil has finished heating, fry the egg rolls in batches of
    2-3 at a time. Fry for 1-2 minutes per side, until they are nice and
    golden brown. Remove to paper towels to drain.
