Ingredients

- 1 cup sour cream

- 1 cup mayonnaise

- 2/3 cup buttermilk

- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce

- 1/2 teaspoon granulated sugar

- 1/2 teaspoon dry mustard

- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

- 1/2 teaspoon white pepper

- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, or to taste

- 4 ounces blue cheese, finely crumbled

![](media/image1.png){width="3.0in" height="3.0055555555555555in"}

Directions

1.  Whisk together the sour cream, mayonnaise, buttermilk,
    Worchestershire sauce, sugar, mustard, garlic powder, pepper and
    salt.

2.  Once thoroughly mixed, stir in the crumbled blue cheese.

3.  Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve. The dressing can be used
    right away, or for even better flavor, refrigerate for at least 8
    hours before serving.

Storage

Blue cheese dressing can be stored in a tightly sealed container in the
refrigerator for up to five days.
