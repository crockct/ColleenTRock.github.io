**Irish Soda Bread (from Mom's Admissions Colleague, Nancy Bannon**)

 

 

Mix:
- 1 c. sour cream
- 1 egg
- ¾ c sugar
- 1 c raisins (I use soft baking raisins, but any are fine.)

 

In a separate bowl, mix:
- 2c flour
- ¼ tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp baking powder 
- ¼ tsp salt

Directions

1. Mix both mixtures together thoroughly.

2. Put in pan that is greased and floured.

3. Bake at 350 degrees for 35-40 minutes.
