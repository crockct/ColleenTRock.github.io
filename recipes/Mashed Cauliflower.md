Ingredients

1 head cauliflower\
salt & freshly cracked pepper\
4-6 cloves garlic, peeled\
1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil\
1/3 cup cream\
1/3 cup grated Romano cheese\
Salt & freshly cracked pepper

Directions

1.  Preheat oven to 350°F

2.  Clean cauliflower and break into large flowerette pieces.

3.  Season peeled garlic cloves with salt and pepper

4.  In a large piece of aluminum foil, tightly wrap cauliflower
    flowerettes and garlic cloves

5.  Bake for 45-55 minutes or until tender.

6.  Put into casserole dish and mash to desired consistency (I like a
    little texture to mine).

7.  Add EVOO and cream and mix in well.

8.  Add the grated Romano cheese and season with salt and pepper as
    desired. 

9.  Optional: add chives

Makes 3-4 servings

Can be made ahead and reheated in a 350°F oven until piping hot.\
