Ingredients

- sugar pumpkin, 2-to-3-pounds

- large onion

- 3 Tablespoon butter

- 3 Tablespoons flour

- ½ teaspoon ground cumin

- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper

- 1.5-2 cups vegetable stock

- 10 oz can of mild Rotel diced tomatoes & green chilies

- 2 cups Monterey Jack cheese *(grate it yourself, it melts better!)*

- 2 cups shredded Mozzarella or Oaxaca cheese

- tortilla chips (for serving)

- (optional) chopped cilantro (garnish)

Directions

1.  Slice off the top 1.5 inches of the pumpkin (save for pictures).
    Scoop out the seeds and stringy pulp.

2.  Mince the onion and grate the cheese.

3.  Sauté onion in butter till translucent and a little brown

4.  Sprinkle on flour and cook constantly stirring until smells toasty
    and looks a light brown

5.  Stir in one can of Rotel tomatoes and spices (cumin, cayenne)

6.  Deglaze with vegetable stock, stirring constantly

7.  Preheat oven to 375

8.  Melt in about 4 cups of cheese, roughly half and half mozzarella and
    Monterey Jack

9.  Then put inside hollowed out pumpkin. The pumpkin should be on a
    baking dish.

10. Add about 1" boiling water to the dish.

11. Cover loosely with foil and bake until the pumpkin is tender, about
    1 hour, 20 minutes

12. Remove foil and continue baking until cheese is golden and bubbly,
    20-25 more mins

13. If the cheese isn't browning, broil it at the end to get brown bits

14. Let cool 5-10 minutes before eating
