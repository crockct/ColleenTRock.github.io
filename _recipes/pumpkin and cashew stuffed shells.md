Ingredients

24 jumbo pasta shells

Pumpkin Ricotta Filling

> 1 batch Cashew Ricotta (recipe follows)
>
> 1 can pumpkin puree (15 oz)
>
> 1/2 cup mozzarella cheese
>
> (*vegan:* use Daiya vegan cheese)
>
> 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
>
> 1 tsp dried thyme
>
> ½ tsp dried sage
>
> salt and pepper, to taste

Sage Cream Sauce

> ¾ cup raw cashews (soaked 4-6 hours)
>
> 1 ½ cups *(vegan: almond or soy)* milk
>
> 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
>
> 2 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
>
> 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
>
> salt and pepper (to taste)

Vegan Cashew Ricotta

> 2 cups raw cashews, soaked 4-6 hours
>
> ½ cup water
>
> 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
>
> 2-3 garlic cloves, crushed
>
> 2 tsp lemon juice
>
> salt to taste

Directions

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees.

Prepare the jumbo shells according to packaged directions. Allow shells
to cool while preparing the filling.

In a food processor, blend together the ingredients for the Cashew
Ricotta until it begins to resemble ricotta cheese. Transfer to a bowl.
Add pumpkin puree, vegan cheese, garlic, dried herbs, salt and pepper
and mix to combine. Set aside.

Sage Cream Sauce

Blend cashews with 1 cup of milk in a high-powered blender until
completely smooth. Transfer to a bowl and whisk in remaining
ingredients.

Pour half of the Sage Cream Sauce into a 9x13 baking pan. Stuff shells
with 2-3 tablespoons of pumpkin filling and place in the pan. Top with
remaining cream sauce.

Cover with tin foil and bake for 30 minutes. Remove foil and bake an
additional 10 minutes until sauce is hot and bubbly. Remove from oven
and allow shells to cool slightly before serving.
