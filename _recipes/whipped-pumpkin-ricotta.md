Ingredients

- 4 cups whole milk ricotta

- 1 cup canned pumpkin

- ¼ tsp salt

- ¼ tsp cinnamon

- ¼ tsp lemon zest

- White pepper

- Olive oil

- ¼ roasted sunflower seeds (garnish)

- Bread and/or crackers (for serving)

Directions

1.  Using a countertop mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, add the
    ricotta, canned pumpkin, salt, cinnamon and lemon zest. Whip on
    medium-high for 1-2 minutes, or until mixture is smooth and light in
    texture.

2.  Transfer mixture into a serving dish. Top with white pepper, olive
    oil and roasted sunflower seeds (optional).

3.  Serve with toasted bread and crackers.
