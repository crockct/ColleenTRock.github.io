Ingredients

- Pesto (about 4oz)

- Dried Fried Sage Leaves (2 teaspoons or to taste)

- Pillsbury Thin Crust Pizza

- Grated Parmesan ( ¼ -- ½ cup)

- Pumpkin butter

- Bacon Strips (half package)

Directions

1.  Spread pumpkin filling on half of the Pillsbury pie crust and spread
    the pesto on the other half.

2.  Make sure both the bacon and sage is crumbled to liking and then
    layer over pumpkin filling.

3.  On the side with pesto add shredded cheese.

4.  Gently roll the Pillsbury crust as tightly as possible in direction
    that will show both sides when cut.

5.  Place the crust in plastic wrap and put in the freezer.

6.  Cut the crust and filling into slices and place on a baking sheet.

7.  Pre-heat the oven to 400 degrees. Line baking sheet with parchment
    paper, place on baking sheet and bake approximately 20 minutes or
    until desired.
