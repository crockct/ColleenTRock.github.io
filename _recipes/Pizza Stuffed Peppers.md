Ingredients

Serves 2, scales easily

1 bell pepper

3 Tbsp cream cheese

Italian Seasoning

Shredded Mozzarella cheese

Pizza Sauce

Parmesan Cheese

Directions

Cut a bell pepper in half. \
Broil on low skin side up for 6-8 minutes depending on how cooked you\'d
like it. \
Flip peppers over, stuff each half with:

- 1½ Tbsp cream cheese

- Italian herb mix

- Shredded mozzarella cheese

- 1-2 Tbsp pizza sauce

Top with shredded Parmesan cheese. \
Broil for 3 more minutes. \
